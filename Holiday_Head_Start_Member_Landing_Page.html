FAQ

Questions before you save your spot?

A few quick answers before Holiday Head Start begins.

Who is this challenge for?

Holiday Head Start is designed for current paid MacroFit members. This registration page makes sure challenge communication is sent separately from your regular MacroFit updates.

When does the challenge run?

It starts Monday, October 19, 2026 and ends Sunday, November 29, 2026. All challenge communication is scheduled in Central Time.

Why do I need to register if I am already a MacroFitter?

Registration gives us permission to send the Holiday Head Start-specific texts, emails, app details, and reminders without mixing them into your normal MacroFit communication.

What if I have a busy week or miss a day?

That is part of the point. Come back to the next choice, use the app and community for support, and keep going. Consistency over perfection.

What if I am not a MacroFit member yet?

The full challenge experience is reserved for current members. Build My MacroFit Plan here.
