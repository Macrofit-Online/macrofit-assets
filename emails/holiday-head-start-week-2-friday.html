Halloween Game Plan

Hey {{contact.first_name}},

Enjoy it. Keep the pattern.

You do not need to “earn” Halloween food. You also do not need to act like one night means the plan no longer exists.

Both things can be true:

You can

Enjoy something you actually want.

You can

Still care about how you feel tomorrow.

That is the point

That is what a real-life plan looks like.

If you are trick-or-treating, going to a party, or handing out candy at home, keep it simple. Eat your normal meals. Decide what is worth having. Enjoy it without turning it into a test of your willpower. Then return to your normal routine at the next meal or the next day.

No guilt. No compensation workout. No “I already messed up, so I might as well…” spiral.
