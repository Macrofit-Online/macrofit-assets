Plan for real life.

Week 2 is here, and we are practicing one of the most useful skills you can have around food: planning for real life instead of acting surprised by it.

The reality

Halloween is coming. Candy will exist. You might have a party, a kid’s event, leftovers on the counter, or a very convincing mini Snickers situation. None of that means you are “off track.”

This week’s question is not, “How can I avoid every fun food?”

It is: What is actually worth it to me, and what do I want my plan to look like around it?

This week’s challenge

Halloween Game Plan

Decide now:

1

Which treats or events are genuinely worth enjoying.

2

What meals you will keep normal.

3

What you will do after Halloween: return to your usual plan instead of turning one night into a long weekend of starting over later.

The goal is not to be strict. The goal is to be intentional enough that you can enjoy the night without guilt or a Monday punishment plan.
