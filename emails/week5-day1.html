Why it matters

Why strength training this week?

Four weeks into a slight calorie deficit, your body is in a really important window. Strength training right now signals to your body to hold onto muscle while it burns fat.

Remember how important it is to keep our muscle? It's why we don't do these crazy, restrictive diets that take our calories too low and our muscle along with it — because that slows your metabolism and changes how your results look and feel long term.
